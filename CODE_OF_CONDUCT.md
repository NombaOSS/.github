# Code of conduct

NombaOSS follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/). Everyone taking part in NombaOSS issues, pull requests, discussions and other project spaces is expected to follow it.

In short: be respectful and welcoming, assume good intent, accept constructive feedback, and don't harass, insult or demean anyone.

## Reporting

Report unacceptable behaviour to the maintainers at security@nomba.com, with "Code of conduct" in the subject. Reports are handled in confidence. Maintainers may remove comments, commits or contributions, and may block contributors, according to the Covenant's enforcement guidelines.
